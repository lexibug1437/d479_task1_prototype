Transportation

Air Travel :

Almost all of our visitors arrive in Taniti by air. Taniti is served by a small airport that can accommodate small jets and propeller planes.

Coming soon - Taniti is in the process of expanding the airport so that larger jets will be able to land on the island within the next few years!

Ground Transportation:

Public Buses

Public buses serve Taniti City.
Running Hours : 5 a.m. to 11 p.m - Daily

Private Transportation:

Private buses and taxis are availabe as needed at any hours.

Rental Cars

Rental cars can be rented from the local rental agency near the airport. They also have car share available through their app!
Visit their website for more info:

WWW.taniticarrental.com

Green Transportation

Bikes and helmets are available to rent from several vendors

- keep in mind, helmets are required by law!

Taniti is flat and very walkable. Visit Merriton Landing for walkable shops, food, and fun!

Great for guests who arrive on Taniti by cruise ship.
